General infomrations about Brawlers:

Shelly is the Starting Brawler that is unlocked upon beginning the game. She was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. She has moderately high health, a high damage output at close range, a long range, and a fast movement speed. Her attack is a burst of shotgun shells that deal high damage up close. Her Super pierces and knocks surviving enemies back and destroys obstacles, similarly dealing high damage up close. Her first Gadget, Fast Forward, allows her to dash a few tiles forward and instantly reloads all of her ammo. Her second Gadget, Clay Pigeons, narrows the width and increases the range of the next 3 attacks. Her first Star Power, Shell Shock, makes her Super momentarily slow enemies down. Her second Star Power, Band-Aid, gives her a bar that charges over time and, when fully charged, heals her when her health falls below 40%. Her Hypercharge, Double Barrel, increases the spread and number of projectiles her Super fires.

Colt is a Rare Brawler who has moderately low health, a long range, a very fast reload speed, and an inconsistently high burst damage. He was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. His attack is a long-ranged volley of six bullets. His Super is a longer-ranged volley of twelve piercing bullets that can destroy obstacles. His first Gadget, Speedloader, causes him to fire two rapid shots that slow down enemies. His second Gadget, Silver Bullet, makes his next attack a single piercing bullet that destroys obstacles and deals the damage of two of his main attack's bullets. His first Star Power, Slick Boots, passively increases his movement speed. His second Star Power, Magnum Special, increases his main attack's range and bullet speed. His Hypercharge, Dual Wielding, increases the width of his Super.

Nita is a Rare Brawler who has moderately high health, a moderate damage output, a very fast reload speed, and attacks with shockwaves that have a medium range and pierce through enemies, being able to hit multiple of them at the same time. She was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. She is most notable for her Super's ability to summon Bruce, a bear that has high health and quick melee attacks that deal very low damage. Her first Gadget, Bear Paws, will briefly stun all enemies in an area around Bruce. Her second Gadget, Faux Fur, shields Bruce for a brief amount of time. Her first Star Power, Bear with Me, heals Bruce slightly but considerably whenever she herself hits an enemy with her attack, and heals her likewise whenever Bruce hits an enemy. Her second Star Power, Hyper Bear, increases Bruce's attack speed. Her Hypercharge, Hyperbearing, increases Bruce's health and movement speed.

Bull is a Rare Brawler who has high health, a normal reload speed, great mobility with his Super and his fast movement speed, a medium range, and a high damage output at close range. He was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. His Trait allows him to charge his Super from enemies damaging him. His attack is a shot of multiple shotgun shells that deal high damage up close. His Super makes him charge in a certain direction, damaging enemies and destroying obstacles in his way, allowing him to get closer to or escape from enemies. His first Gadget, T-Bone Missile, partially heals him after hitting an enemy Brawler. His second Gadget, Stomper, allows Bull to briefly slow enemies down; if the enemies are already slowed, it will stun them instead. His first Star Power, Berserker, doubles his reload speed when he falls below 60% health. His second Star Power, Tough Guy, shields him when he falls below 30% health. His Hypercharge, Jaws of Steel, allows his Super to gain a significant damage-reduction shield while it's active.

Brock is a Rare Brawler who has low health, a long range, and a high damage output, but a moderately slow reload speed. He was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. His attack shoots long-ranged rockets that explode and deal area damage in a short radius, leaving a fire puddle. His Super fires a barrage of rockets in a large area, destroying obstacles and dealing massive damage. His first Gadget, Rocket Laces, allows him to fire at his feet, damaging nearby enemies and knock them back, and launching Brock into the air. His second Gadget, Rocket Fuel, makes Brock's next attack a mega rocket that has a larger explosion radius, destroys walls and travels faster. His first Star Power, More Rockets!, increases the number of rockets fired from his Super. His second Star Power, Rocket No. Four, increases Brock's ammo capacity to 4. His Hypercharge, Rocket Barrage, increases the number of rockets fired from his next Super, which deal high damage.

El Primo is a Rare Brawler that has the second-highest health of any Brawler, a very fast reload speed, a high damage output, and great mobility with his Super and his fast movement speed, but a short attack range. He was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. El Primo's Trait allows him to charge his Super from enemies damaging him. He attacks with a flurry of close-ranged punches that deal high total damage. His Super is a jump that upon landing, deals damage, knocks enemies back, and destroys cover. El Primo's first Gadget, Suplex Supplement, instantly dashes forward, grabbing enemies and throwing them behind him. His second Gadget, Asteroid Belt, creates a projectile-destroying shield around him temporarily. His first Star Power, El Fuego, sets enemies hit by his Super on fire. His second Star Power, Meteor Rush, momentarily increases his movement speed after using his Super. His Hypercharge, Gravity Leap, allows his Super to pull in enemies upon hitting them.

Barley is a Rare Brawler who has low health and slow reload speed, but a high burst damage potential and a long range. He was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. He attacks by lobbing a bottle of harmful liquid that splashes onto the ground and creates a small puddle on the ground that lasts for a period of time, dealing damage to enemies splashed and inside the puddle over time. His Super lobs five similar bottles on fire. These bottles travel further, cover a greater area, persist for a longer period of time, and they do not set a burning effect. His first Gadget, Sticky Syrup Mixer, creates a sticky puddle around himself that slows enemies inside it down. His second Gadget, Herbal Tonic, throws bottles around himself and teammates that heal them if they stand on the puddles. His first Star Power, Medical Use, heals him slightly when he lobs his bottle with his attack. His second Star Power, Extra Noxious, increases his main attack's damage per second. His Hypercharge, Bottled-Up Rage, makes his Super lob three big bottles of liquid that covers a larger area than his normal Super, destroying bushes in the process. Barley and his Wizard skin can be unlocked for free by linking an account to Supercell ID.

Poco is a Rare Brawler who has moderately high health, a medium range, and the ability to provide immense healing to his teammates, but very low damage. He was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. For his attack, Poco plays his guitarrón, sending damaging musical notes within a wide spread and range that deals minimal damage and heal teammates for a small amount. His Super fires a larger and faster musical wave, covering a longer distance and healing himself and any teammates in a larger amount within its range. His first Gadget, Tuning Fork, gives out a circular musical wave around him that allows himself and teammates in it to heal over a few seconds. His second Gadget, Protective Tunes, cleanses teammates from any adverse status effects and protects teammates from those effects momentarily in an throwable area-of-effect. His first Star Power, Da Capo!, increases the amount of healing granted by his main attack. His second Star Power, Screeching Solo, allows his Super to deal moderately low damage to enemies it hits. His Hypercharge, Medic's Melody, makes his Super overheal teammates with a shield that decays over time.

Jessie is a Super Rare Brawler who has moderate health and damage output, a moderately low reload speed, and immense utility with her Super. She was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. Her attack is a long-ranged electric orb that bounces between enemies, dealing less damage per bounce. Her Super places Scrappy, a turret with moderate health and damage output that excels at attacking, distracting, and defending. Her first Gadget, Spark Plug, creates a shockwave around Scrappy that slows down enemies. Her second Gadget, Recoil Spring, doubles Scrappy's attack speed for 5 seconds. Her first Star Power, Energize, allows her to heal her turret by attacking it and her second Star Power, Shocky, allows her turret to shoot mini energy orbs that can bounce between enemies. Her Hypercharge, Scrappy 2.0, increases the health and damage of her next Super.

Dynamike is a Super Rare Brawler who has moderately low health, a fast reload speed, a high damage output and an above-average movement speed. He was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. He attacks by lobbing sticks of dynamite over obstacles at a long range that blow up and deal high damage in a medium radius. For his Super, he lobs a large barrel of dynamite that deals very high damage and knocks surviving enemies back in a large radius while also destroying obstacles. His first Gadget, Fidget Spinner, allows him to spin with a speed boost and rapidly fire sticks of dynamite in a large radius around himself. His second Gadget, Satchel Charge, makes Dynamike's next main attack stun enemies briefly. His first Star Power, Dyna-Jump, allows his attack, Super and Hypercharge to propel him over obstacles and Brawlers. His second Star Power, Demolition, allows his Super to deal more damage. His Hypercharge, Boomer, spawns multiple sticks of dynamite from his Super upon detonation in every direction that blow up and deal high damage in a small radius.

Rico (formerly Ricochet) is a Super Rare Brawler with moderately low health but a very fast reload speed and a high burst damage potential. He was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. Both his attack and Super fire long-ranged bullets that can bounce off walls and continue traveling. However, his Super travels further and pierces, dealing damage to multiple enemies at once, though the bullets can only hit the target once. If used correctly, Rico can defeat enemies around walls without coming under enemy fire. His first Gadget, Multiball Launcher, deploys a vending machine that provides cover for him and his teammates, and blasts waves of bouncy bullets all around it upon being destroyed. His second Gadget, Multiball, fires a large bullet that splits into 3 smaller bullets upon hitting a wall or enemy Brawlers. His first Star Power, Super Bouncy, grants extra damage to his bullets from his attack or Super if they bounce. His second Star Power, Robo Retreat, increases his movement speed when he is at low health. His Hypercharge, Trick Shot King, allows the projectiles from his Super to bounce much further than normal.

Bo is an Epic Brawler who has moderate health, immense utility with his Super, and a high damage output at close range, in exchange for a moderately slow reload speed. He was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. He attacks by firing three long-ranged arrows that explode in a small radius at the end of their range. His Super places three traps that can be triggered to explode a moment later by being walked on by an enemy, damaging and knocking enemies back upon exploding. His first Gadget, Super Totem, places a totem that increases Bo's and his allies' Super charging rate while inside its area-of-effect. His second Gadget, Tripwire, manually detonates his traps. His first Star Power, Circling Eagle, increases his field of view into bushes. His second Star Power, Snare a Bear, causes his traps to stun enemies momentarily instead of knocking them back. His Hypercharge, Catch a Bear, makes his Super deploy five traps.

Mortis is a Mythic Brawler who has moderately high health, a moderate damage output, a short range, and great mobility with his attack and his very fast movement speed, at the expense of having a very slow reload speed. He was one of the 15 Brawlers who released at the first Beta launch. He attacks by swinging his shovel and dashing a few tiles forward, dealing damage to enemies in his path. His first Gadget, Combo Spinner, instantly spins his shovel around him, dealing damage to nearby enemies. His second Gadget, Creature of the Night, allows Mortis to pass over walls and become immune for a short time. His first Star Power, Creepy Harvest, heals him whenever he defeats an enemy Brawler. His second Star Power, Coiled Snake, reduces the time needed to charge his attack bar. His Hypercharge, Blood Boomerang, increases his Super's projectile speed and makes Mortis' Super return back to him, dealing a second wave of damage and healing.
