INFORMATIONS ABOUT REMOVED CONTENT:

Buzz Lightyear was a Time Limited Legendary Brawler who could be unlocked as a Brawlidays 2024 gift from December 12th to December 31st and could be played during the Pizza Planet event until February 4th, 2025. Buzz Lightyear had three modes. In his first mode, he has a moderately low health, moderately high damage output, a long range, and a fast reload speed. Buzz Lightyear's attack is a laser blast that will also damage enemies over time upon contact. For his Super, he fires five lasers from left to right, that will similarly damage enemies over time upon hitting them. In his second mode, Buzz has high health, a moderately high damage output, a very fast movement speed, but a short range. Buzz attacks by swiping his saber in a wide and close-ranged arc. His Super is a jump in the air which allows him to leap over walls, and upon landing, deals damage, and knocks enemies back. In his third mode, Buzz attacks by firing two lasers that deal more damage up close. For his Super, he flies above the ground while dropping bombs onto enemies that deal area damage. Buzz's Gadget, Turbo Boosters, allows him to dash a few tiles in the direction he is facing. His Hypercharge, Bravado, gives Buzz temporary buffs.
